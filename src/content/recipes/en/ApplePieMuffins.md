steps:
  - For the crumble, briefly knead together 84 g sugar, 84 g margarine, 129 g flour and 1.5 tsp. cinnamon. Place in the
    fridge.
  - For the shortcrust dough, knead 30 g powdered sugar, 183 g flour, 120 g margarine and 1 pinch of salt by hand into a
    smooth dough.
  - Line all 12 cups of a muffin tin with paper liners. Press a generous portion of shortcrust dough into each - base
    about 1 cm thick, rim about 1 cm high.
  - Peel the apples and cut into small cubes. Mix with the brown sugar and apple sauce and distribute evenly over all 12
    bases.
  - Distribute the crumble evenly over all 12 muffins.
  - "Bake in a preheated oven (conventional: 200°C / fan: 175°C / 390°F) for about 20 - 25 minutes, until the crumble is
    nicely golden-brown. Let cool completely before removing the muffins from the tin - the shortcrust base breaks
    easily while warm."
