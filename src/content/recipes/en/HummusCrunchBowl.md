steps:
  - Crush the cucumbers - slice them once lengthwise and smash them with the flat side of your knife, then
    break up the pieces or cut them into bite-sized chunks. Add to a large bowl.
  - Finely dice the bell pepper, dice the red onion, and halve the small tomatoes. Add everything to the bowl.
  - Drain the chickpeas and add them to the bowl.
  - Rip the tofu into small chunks by hand and add it in.
  - Crush the garlic with the flat side of your knife, peel it, then finely chop or grate it into the bowl
    (1-3 cloves, to taste).
  - Add the 200 g hummus, a generous splash of lemon juice, and season with salt, pepper, smoked paprika, and
    1 Tbsp. curry powder.
  - Optionally add a handful of cashews or peanuts for extra crunch, a drizzle of sesame oil, and some grated
    or finely diced ginger.
  - Mix everything together thoroughly until the hummus coats all the ingredients. Season to taste - salt and
    curry are the main seasonings here, so adjust until it tastes right. Serve and enjoy.
